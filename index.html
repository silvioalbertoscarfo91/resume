About

I'm a Senior Full-Stack Engineer at ti8m in Bern. Since 2019 I've worked on DaziT, the programme that digitises the processes of the Federal Office for Customs and Border Security (BAZG). I started on mobile apps, moved across the stack, and since 2024 I've been Lead Engineer of Raporta.

Most of my day sits where business meets engineering: turning requirements into an architecture and a plan, weighing risks with stakeholders, then reviewing code and pairing so the team ships with confidence.

Outside the programme I build to learn: a concurrency-focused banking platform, a fully local LLM pipeline, and agent-driven, spec-first delivery experiments with Claude Code.
