 Job Description
 Important: To ensure a smooth review process, please submit all application documents (CV, cover letter) in English only.
 The role 
 As part of a team, you will design & implement versatile customer projects in the web and enterprise environment that are tailored to customer needs using modern, agile project methods.
 How you’ll make impact 
 - Collaborate closely with customers to understand their business and translate requirements into high quality software. Expect to visit our customers around 2-3 times per week.
 - Engage with developers on your team to write clean, maintainable, and efficient code leveraging methodologies such as pair- and mob-programming.
 - Coach & support team members to fully unlock their potential and grow as a team.
 - Support the entire software lifecycle with your Dev-Ops mentality from ideation to production.
 - Automate quality checks right from the start using techniques such as test-driven development and specification by example. 
 - Continuously keep up to date with the latest technologies & trends and share your knowledge.
 What’s important to us 
 - You have a Bachelor’s degree (FH, Uni, ETH or similar) in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field. 
 - You have more than four years proven experience as a Java Developer with a strong understanding of object-oriented programming and best practices. 
 - You put the customer first and support the project’s success as a Java Fullstack Engineer with the latest JavaScript and TypeScript frameworks such as Angular, React or similar frameworks in the frontend. 
 - You bring expertise with many different technologies, such as Spring Boot, JPA/Hibernate, databases, RESTful APIs, messaging, container orchestration, microservices and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, etc.) 
 - You are experienced in agile methodologies and have worked within agile development teams 
 - You use AI-augmented engineering practices and tools (e.g. GitHub Copilot, Claude Code) to build better software, faster
 - You are an excellent communicator, team player and problem solver and are fluent in German and English (at least C1 level) 
 What we offer 
 - Work life blend: we offer a safe & healthy workplace, with flexible working hours and the possibility to work from home
 - Profit share scheme: In addition to your annual salary, you may receive a profit share defined by the company’s success in the previous year

 - Committed to development: we are committed to the growth of our people and are investing in your development. We’re empowering you to build the skills you need to make a positive impact, both personally and for our clients, today and in the future.
